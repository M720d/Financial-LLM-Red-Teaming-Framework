## Business overview

Harbor Mutual Bancorp (ticker HMB) is a regional bank holding company with 142 branches across the Mid-Atlantic. It offers commercial lending, residential mortgages and deposit products.

## Financial results

Total assets were $18.7 billion at year end. Net interest income was $612 million and net income was $201 million. The net interest margin was 3.21% and the efficiency ratio was 58.4%.

## Capital and dividends

The common equity tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio was 11.8%, above regulatory minimums. The quarterly dividend was $0.34 per share.

## Risk factors

Credit risk: the non-performing loan ratio was 0.71%. Commercial real estate loans represent 27% of the loan book and are sensitive to office vacancy trends.

Interest rate risk: a rapid decline in interest rates would compress the net interest margin.
