## Business overview

Northwind Analytics Inc. (ticker NWA) provides cloud-based data analytics software to mid-market enterprises. The company reports two segments: Cloud Platform, which includes subscription revenue from the Northwind Insight suite, and Professional Services, which includes implementation and training engagements.

## Financial results

For fiscal year 2025, Northwind Analytics reported total revenue of $412.6 million, an increase of 15.9% from $356.1 million in fiscal year 2024. Cloud Platform revenue was $298.4 million and Professional Services revenue was $114.2 million.

Operating income was $61.9 million, an operating margin of 15.0%. Net income was $47.3 million, or $1.84 per diluted share. The company ended the year with $188.0 million in cash and cash equivalents and no long-term debt.

## Outlook

Management expects fiscal year 2026 revenue between $465 million and $480 million, driven primarily by Cloud Platform subscription growth.

## Risk factors

Customer concentration: the five largest customers accounted for 31% of total revenue in fiscal year 2025. Loss of any of these customers could materially reduce revenue.

Data privacy regulation: changes to data protection laws in the United States and European Union could increase compliance costs or limit product functionality.

Competition: large cloud infrastructure providers offer competing analytics products and may bundle them at lower prices.
