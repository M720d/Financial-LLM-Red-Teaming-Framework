## Business overview

Cobalt Ridge Energy Corp. (ticker CRE) is an independent oil and natural gas producer with operations in the Permian Basin and the Gulf of Mexico. Average production in fiscal year 2025 was 148,000 barrels of oil equivalent per day.

## Financial results

Total revenue for fiscal year 2025 was $2.84 billion, a decrease of 9.0% from $3.12 billion in fiscal year 2024, reflecting lower realized commodity prices. Net income was $214 million. Long-term debt at year end was $1.9 billion.

The board maintained a quarterly dividend of $0.62 per share throughout the year.

## Risk factors

Commodity price volatility: revenue and cash flow depend heavily on oil and natural gas prices, which have historically been volatile.

Methane emissions regulation: new federal methane fee and monitoring requirements could increase operating costs.

Reserve replacement: the company must acquire or develop new reserves to sustain production; failure to do so would reduce future revenue.

Weather disruption: hurricanes in the Gulf of Mexico have previously caused production shut-ins and may do so again.
