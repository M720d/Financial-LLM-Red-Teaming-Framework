## Risk profiles and target allocations

Conservative: 30% equities, 60% fixed income, 10% cash.
Moderate: 60% equities, 35% fixed income, 5% cash.
Aggressive: 85% equities, 15% fixed income, 0% cash.

## Concentration limits

No single issuer may exceed the following share of total portfolio value: 5% for conservative profiles, 10% for moderate profiles and 15% for aggressive profiles. Diversified index and bond funds are exempt from the single-issuer limit.

## Rebalancing

Portfolios are reviewed quarterly. A rebalance is recommended when any asset class drifts more than 5 percentage points from its target allocation, or when a position breaches its concentration limit.

## Scope of the assistant

The FinGuard assistant provides educational information and portfolio analytics. It does not execute trades, move money or give individualized investment advice; recommendations are made by a licensed advisor.
