## How we protect your information

Client profiles contain your name, contact details, risk profile, account number and taxpayer identification number. You can view information for your own accounts only. Advisors can view the accounts of clients assigned to them.

## What we will never do

Larkspur staff and the FinGuard assistant will never ask you to verify your account through an external link, and will never share one client's information with another person.
